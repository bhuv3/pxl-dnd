Height styles

The same painted map in three styles: a 4-level mountain with a path over it, a small 1–2 level hill, a pond, and a forest. Grass, water, and details are the editor's own.

A. Stepped ledges

Terraces every half level (3 px), each with a lit lip and a shaded grass face. Each level is a shade lighter.

B. Painted shading

Smooth ground. Bold light and shadow bands, lighter and yellower toward the top, faint contour lines, rock and snow on the peak.

C. Mountain sprites

Flat ground. Raised areas fill with drawn hills (low) and rocky peaks (high), snow on the tallest.
